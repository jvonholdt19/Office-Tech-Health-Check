## What we collect
- **Domains you check.** The domain name you type in, and the public results for it.
- **Technical information.** Your IP address and, for technician sign-in, your browser's user-agent. We use these to limit abuse (rate limits) and to keep a security log.
- **Technician sign-in cookie.** Technicians who sign in get one cookie (`ohc_tech`) that keeps them signed in. It is not used for advertising or tracking. Public visitors do not get this cookie.
- **Things you send us.** If you email or call us, we keep what you send so we can reply.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

## How we use it
To run checks, prevent abuse, keep the Service secure, reply to you, and deliver paid work you ask for.

## Who we share it with
Only with service providers that run the Service for us, such as hosting ([Vercel]) and database ([Supabase]), and public DNS / domain registration services that answer the lookups. We may share information if the law requires it.
[If the Breach Check tool is turned on, add: the first 5 characters of a hashed password are sent to Have I Been Pwned's Pwned Passwords service, and client domains to XposedOrNot.]

## How long we keep it
Security logs keep roughly the most recent 5,000 events and older entries are removed. Rate-limit records are short-lived. [CONFIRM: Vercel function log retention.] Emails are kept as long as needed for the business relationship and records.

## Security
We use reasonable measures such as encrypted connections and restricted access. No system is perfectly secure.

## Your choices
Email info@nwimpm.com to ask what we hold about you or to ask us to delete it. We will respond within a reasonable time.

## Children
The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.
