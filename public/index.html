    What we check

    Three checks, run from the outside using public information.

    Email security

    • SPF, DMARC and DKIM records
    • Which email provider the domain uses
    • Protection against spoofed email sent in your name
    • Domain registration and website certificate basics

    Website security

    • HTTPS, redirects, certificate validity and expiry
    • Security headers: HSTS, CSP, clickjacking protection, nosniff
    • Cookie flags and mixed content on the homepage
    • Software versions on show, including the WordPress version

    Domain health

    • Renewal date, transfer lock and domain status
    • Nameservers, including ones that don't answer for the domain
    • SOA, CAA, MX and DNSSEC records
    • Forgotten DNS records that point at services which no longer exist

    A growing suite of tools for your business technology.

    How it works

    1. We run passive checks

      Public DNS, the domain registry (RDAP) and a single visit to your homepage. Nothing is installed: no logins, no scans of your network.

    2. You get a clear report

      A letter grade, red, yellow and green findings, and why each one matters, in plain English.

    3. We help you fix it

      Step-by-step guides for common setups (Cloudflare, WordPress, Apache, Nginx, IIS and website builders), a ready-to-send message for your web host, and a printable PDF.

    What you get

    Every finding comes with a status, the reason it matters and, where there's something to change, a guide for your setup and a message you can forward to whoever looks after your website.

    • A letter grade with red, yellow and green findings
    • Step-by-step "How to fix" guides
    • A ready-to-send message for your web host
    • A printable PDF of the report

    Built on recognised standards

    Our checks follow guidance from:

    • Mozilla HTTP Observatory scoring (website headers)
    • OWASP Secure Headers Project and ASVS (headers and cookies)
    • Qualys SSL Labs rating guide (certificates and TLS)
    • IETF RFCs: SPF (RFC 7208), DMARC (RFC 7489), DKIM (RFC 6376), DNS (RFC 1034, 1912, 2182), CAA (RFC 8659), null MX (RFC 7505)
    • ICANN policies (EPP status codes, expired registration recovery)
